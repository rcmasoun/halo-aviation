Frequently Asked Questions

Which brands is HALO an authorized dealer for?

We hold authorized dealerships for Hoffmann Group, Kennedy, Cromwell, DeWalt, Bison Life, Stahlwille, STPL, Groz, Stanley, Holex and Garant — covering tools, machines and measuring instruments for MROs.

Can HALO manufacture custom tooling or MHE to our drawings?

Yes. As a manufacturer, our team designs, researches, develops and fabricates jigs, MHE, jacks & lifting equipment, wooden shorings and other customised solutions for the aviation industry.

Do you supply outside the aviation MRO industry?

Our focus and expertise since 2020 has been supporting India's aviation MRO industry, so get in touch and we'll let you know if we can help with your requirement.

Where is HALO Technocrat based?

We're based in Ludhiana, Punjab, and supply and ship to MROs and airlines across India.
